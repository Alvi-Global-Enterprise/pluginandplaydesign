Privacy Policy

Plug And Play Design values and respects the privacy of its online users. Maintaining your confidentiality and safeguarding your personal information is a top priority. This Privacy Policy outlines Plug And Play Design’ commitment to protecting user information and the measures taken to ensure complete security and confidentiality at all times.

  • 01

    Motive

    The purpose of this Privacy Policy is to explain why Plug And Play Design collects and stores information from users and customers, the types of data collected, and the limited scenarios in which this information may be shared with third parties. It also clarifies how the collected data is used to improve user experience and service quality.

  • 02

    Gathering Exclusive Particulars

    Plug And Play Design collects specific types of information from its users and customers for various purposes. The details of the data collected are outlined below:

  • 03

    Mainstream Queries and Remarks

    • Name
    • Email
    • Phone/Cell Number
    • Address
    • City
    • Country

  • 04

    Business Associates Vendors

    • Company/Organization Information
    • Emergency/Support Contact Information

  • 05

    Job Hunters

    • Unique Identification Information
    • Educational/Academic Background
    • Employment/Work History

  • 06

    Use of Collected Information

    As stated above, the purpose of this Privacy Policy is to clarify how Plug And Play Design collects and utilizes information. The data collected from users, customers, vendors, business associates, and job applicants is used to improve website performance, communication, and overall experience.

    Our design, development, and quality assurance teams regularly monitor and maintain the site’s interface and performance. For this reason, users and stakeholders may occasionally receive updates or notifications related to website maintenance, technological improvements, or performance enhancements.

    Plug And Play Design may share limited data with trusted associates to maintain the site’s performance. All such sharing is conducted securely and only when necessary.

    Information collected from job applicants is used exclusively to assess their qualifications, experience, and eligibility for potential employment at Plug And Play Design. Such information may be shared internally with relevant departments or managers, always ensuring strict confidentiality and privacy throughout the recruitment process.

  • 07

    Third-Party Sharing

    Plug And Play Design places great emphasis on protecting user information and only shares it with third parties when necessary to:

    • Enhance user experience on the website
    • Fulfill promotional or communication needs
    • Maintain efficient communication with customers and business associates

    Any data shared with third parties is done so under formal contractual agreements between Plug And Play Design and those entities. Third parties are strictly prohibited from disclosing, sharing, or using the provided data beyond the agreed purpose.

  • 08

    Security of Exclusive Information

    Plug And Play Design has implemented robust security measures to ensure the complete protection and confidentiality of all gathered data. Maintaining user privacy is of utmost importance, and our website is safeguarded against any unauthorized access or interruptions.

    The site is built on a secure HTML5-based eCommerce infrastructure and protected with the latest Secure Sockets Layer (SSL) certification. This technology encrypts all submitted data, ensuring the safe and secure storage of user, customer, vendor, and business associate information.

  • 09

    The Use of Cookies

    Plug And Play Design uses cookies to gather additional information that enhances user experience. These may include details such as user demographics, browsing behavior, website interactions, and preferences regarding the overall look and feel of the website.

    Cookies are alphanumeric identifiers stored on the user’s computer or device through their web browser. They help recognize browser types and remember user interactions from prior visits, enabling a smoother browsing experience.

    Users can manage cookie preferences through their browser settings, including enabling, disabling, or receiving alerts for new cookies. However, Plug And Play Design recommends enabling cookies for optimal website functionality, as some features may depend on them.

    By allowing cookies, users ensure full access to all interactive and enhanced features offered on the Plug And Play Design website.
